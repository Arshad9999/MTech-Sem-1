Suggested study loop

Four passes, increasing depth.

01 · Orient

Read the syllabus map and define every unfamiliar term in your own words.

02 · Derive

Reproduce the equations and explain the assumptions that make them valid.

03 · Apply

Work the examples without looking, then vary an input or assumption.

04 · Defend

Explain why the chosen method fits and what could invalidate the result.
